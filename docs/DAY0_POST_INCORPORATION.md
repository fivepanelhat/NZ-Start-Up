# Day-0 Post-Incorporation Sequence

Practical sequence after a New Zealand limited company becomes **Registered** on the Companies Register. No specific date is assumed — use on incorporation day and the following week.

## 1. Certificate of Incorporation + Company Extract

**What you need**
- **Certificate of Incorporation** — official proof the company exists and the formation date. Required by banks, IRD, Xero, and most suppliers.
- **Company Extract** — current snapshot of name, company number, NZBN, registered office, directors, and shareholding. Free and live.

**How**
1. Go to [companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz)
2. Search by exact company name or company number from the confirmation email.
3. On the company page select **Company Extract** → View / Download PDF or email it.
4. Certificate of Incorporation is usually available as a downloadable PDF on the same page once status shows Registered.

**Tips**
- Download both immediately and store in a dedicated legal folder (plus email copies).
- Banks almost always ask for a Company Extract dated within ~30 days.
- NZBN is issued automatically — note it from the extract.

**Time:** 5–10 minutes once the company is live.

## 2. Company IRD number + GST

### IRD number (mandatory for a company)
- A limited company requires its **own** non-individual IRD number.
- Apply online via IRD’s non-individual IRD application as soon as you have the Certificate of Incorporation.
- Supporting document: Certificate of Incorporation (PDF).
- Processing: often a few days to ~10–20 working days if further information is needed.

### GST registration
- **Compulsory** when turnover is (or is expected to be) ≥ $60,000 in a 12-month period, **or** if you start charging GST.
- **Voluntary** registration below the threshold is allowed and is often useful when you will have significant GST on expenses (hardware, software, services) that you want to claim back.
- GST number = the company’s IRD number once registered.
- Choose filing frequency (commonly 2-monthly or 6-monthly for early-stage companies) and accounting basis (Invoice basis is most common).

**Recommended order**
1. Obtain company IRD number.
2. Set up myIR for the company.
3. Register for GST (can be done in the same session once IRD is live).

## 3. Business bank account

**Typical requirements**
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Current Company Extract
- Company IRD number (some banks accept later; most prefer it at application)
- Personal ID for all directors / signatories
- Proof of residential address for directors
- Brief description of activities and expected transaction volume

**Timeline**
- Online application: 15–30 minutes
- Full activation: commonly 3–10 business days (AML checks)

**Practical notes**
- Prioritise banks with reliable **Xero bank feeds** (BNZ, ASB, ANZ, Westpac are commonly used).
- Apply the same day the Certificate is available if possible.
- Start with one operating account; multi-currency can be added later.

## 4. Xero (or equivalent accounting software)

**Why Xero is the common default for NZ startups**
- Native GST calculation and direct filing to IRD
- Strong bank-feed integration with major NZ banks
- Easy accountant invite at no extra cost
- Clean default chart of accounts for limited companies

**Setup sequence (once IRD + bank exist)**
1. Create Xero organisation → company name, NZ, financial year end (usually 31 March).
2. Enter company IRD number and GST details once registered.
3. Connect the business bank account(s).
4. Customise invoice template (logo, bank details, payment terms).
5. Review / simplify the default chart of accounts.
6. Optionally invite an accountant as an Advisor user.

You can create the Xero organisation on incorporation day; full power (feeds + GST) comes online once the bank account is active and GST is registered.

## Suggested same-day order of operations

1. Complete online incorporation + payment  
2. Confirm status = Registered  
3. Download Certificate of Incorporation + Company Extract  
4. Apply for company IRD number (upload Certificate)  
5. Submit business bank account application (attach Certificate + Extract)  
6. Create Xero organisation  
7. Once IRD arrives → finish GST registration and update Xero  

## Hard boundaries for agents / NZ-Start-Up tools

| Agent may | Agent must not |
|-----------|----------------|
| Draft checklists and capture numbers the founder provides | File Companies Office, IRD, or bank applications |
| Prepare GST working papers from bank feeds / Xero snapshots | File myIR returns or pay IRD |
| Remind founder of missing Day-0 items | Invent company numbers, IRD numbers, or bank details |

All filings and payments remain founder-owned via their own RealMe / myIR / bank session.
